TXDownloader Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-05-31 | Version: V1.0

Summary

TXDownloader parses and downloads media from public X/Twitter links that users actively submit. The app does not provide account login, direct-message access, posting, or paid-content bypass. Download history and downloaded files are mainly stored on your device. Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand product health, crashes, and non-fatal failures.

1. Scope

This policy applies to TXDownloader. The app parses and downloads media from public X/Twitter links that users actively submit. It does not provide account registration, social login, posting, direct-message access, or paid-content bypass.

2. Information processed

The app may process public X/Twitter links and share text you enter, paste, or share to the app; temporary public post information needed to show results; local download history; app logs and error information; and crash logs, diagnostics, app interactions, device/app metadata, and product-health signals collected by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

3. How information is used

This information is used only to identify public links you submit, parse downloadable media, save files locally, show download history, support local preview/playback, diagnose failures, improve stability, and understand whether core features are working. We do not use this information for advertising profiles, personalized recommendations, user tracking, or sale.

4. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics is used for aggregate events such as parsing, downloads, history filters, language changes, and notification permission results. Crashlytics is used to record crashes and sanitized non-fatal exceptions. We do not intentionally send raw X/Twitter links, media URLs, cookies, authorization headers, tokens, post text, usernames, file names, or diagnostic log contents as Analytics event parameters or Crashlytics custom keys.

5. Third-party services and network requests

When parsing and downloading media, the app may access the target page, public media resources, and fallback parser services required for a link you submitted. Those websites or services may log requests according to their own rules. Firebase data processing is governed by Google Firebase terms and privacy practices.

6. Local storage, permissions, and deletion

Download history is stored mainly on the device. Downloaded images, videos, and GIFs are saved to the system downloads folder or another media location accessible to the app. Network access is required; foreground-service notifications may be used for background downloads. You can delete history in the app, clear app data in system settings, or delete downloaded files through a file manager.

7. Minors

This app is not directed to children. Minors should use it with guardian guidance and only download content allowed by laws, platform rules, and content owners.

8. Disclaimer and contact

Only download public content you have the right to save and use, and follow the terms, copyright rules, and local laws that apply to X/Twitter and related websites. TXDownloader is not affiliated with X/Twitter and does not encourage or support copyright infringement, illegal distribution, privacy violations, or paid-content bypass. If future features change data processing, this policy will be updated.

Contact

For privacy-related feedback, contact us through TXDownloader GitHub Issues. https://github.com/sharkmabin/TXDownloader/issues
